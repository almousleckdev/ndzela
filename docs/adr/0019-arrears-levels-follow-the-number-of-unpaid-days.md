# ADR-0019: Arrears levels follow the number of unpaid days

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-14

**Context.** The platform escalated by the *age* of the oldest unpaid rent (24 h yellow, 48 h dark yellow, 72 h red and suspension). The previous platform's "Gestion des impayés" screen, which staff act on every day, counts the *unpaid days* (3 or more is critical), and the contract's sanctions table (article 7) also speaks by day (day 1 warning, day 2 urgent reminder and penalty, day 3 remote blocking). During the migration the two disagreed: the previous screen showed 10 critical riders, the new platform 4, and a rider with one old unpaid day was suspended while the old screen showed "1 jour".

**Decision.** The level is a function of the number of unpaid rent days of the active contract: 0 none, 1 `YELLOW` (Rappel), 2 `DARK_YELLOW` (Urgent), 3 or more `RED` (Suspendu). `LATE` stays in the enum but is no longer produced. The warning steps (e-mail, urgent e-mail, e-mail plus automatic suspension and staff notification) are recorded once for the rent day that made the count reach 1, 2 and 3 (`arrears_events`). The penalty rule (a day unpaid for more than 48 hours) is unchanged. The dashboard shows Rappel in yellow, Urgent in red and Suspendu in deep red.

**Consequences.** (+) The new platform and the old screen agree, and so does the contract. (-) A rider is no longer escalated just because one old rent day stays unpaid for a long time; he stays at the reminder level until a second and third day are unpaid. A rider who pays the oldest day drops one level at once. Unpaid days that exist when the previous platform's history is imported must not trigger e-mails or suspensions on their own: see [legacy-import](../migration/legacy-import.md).

**Addendum (2026-10-14): rent awaiting its deadline and real-time refresh.** The deadline of a rent day was the next day at 12:00 (changed to the same day by [ADR-0029](0029-rent-is-due-the-same-day-at-noon.md)), so yesterday's rent is not *unpaid* before noon. The old screen already counts it from midnight, so the platform also exposes it as *awaiting*: `awaitingDays` / `awaitingAmount` per rider (stored on `drivers`, migration V33), `totalAwaiting` in the summary, and `atRisk` = riders under 3 unpaid days who reach 3 at noon if yesterday's rent is not paid. The level and the suspension still follow the unpaid days only (no warning or suspension before noon). "À recouvrer (hier inclus)" = overdue + awaiting equals the old screen's total at any hour, and both screens give the same figures after noon. The arrears job runs every minute, the arrears list is sorted by unpaid days (most first) then oldest overdue, riders who are `INACTIVE` (archived former riders) are left out of the counts, and the dashboard refreshes the arrears and follow-up pages every 15 seconds.
