# ADR-0035: A driver can be erased with their money; half days can be granted for today and the coming week

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-12 · **Amends:** [drivers.md](../business/drivers.md) ("deleting is allowed only while no money was recorded"), [ADR-0031](0031-deleting-sessions-and-recharges-and-the-zella-wallet.md) pattern, the half-day rules in [payments-and-arrears.md](../business/payments-and-arrears.md)

**Context.** Staff need to remove test or mistaken drivers that already have payments or recharges, and to grant a half day for today (contract signed after noon, an emergency) instead of only for a past day after a power cut.

**Decision.**
1. **Purge.** `POST /drivers/{id}/purge {confirm: true}` (permission `drivers:purge`, SUPER_ADMIN and ADMIN by default, assignable to other roles) erases the driver, their contracts, assignments, leaves, files **and the money recorded on them** (ledger entries and the days they settled, recharges with their cancellations; payment attempts, incidents and arrears events follow by cascade). `confirm` must be true: the dashboard shows it as a "Je suis sûr" box. The ordinary `DELETE` keeps refusing a driver with money (`DRIVER_HAS_HISTORY`).
2. **Append-only ledger.** The ledger tables stay append-only; their triggers accept a delete only inside the purge transaction, which switches `app.ledger_purge` on for that transaction alone (same mechanism as `app.charging_delete`, V49; migration V55). The purge is **all or nothing**.
3. **Features erase their own data.** The driver feature publishes a synchronous `DriverPurgeEvent` inside the transaction; `payment` and `charging` listen and delete their rows, reporting what they erased. The audit trail (`DRIVER_PURGED`) keeps a summary: the total of the payments erased and the number of rows per kind.
4. **Several at once.** The drivers table has row selection; "Supprimer la sélection" deletes the selected drivers of the page one by one (each deletion is atomic) and reports the failures. Only holders of `drivers:delete` or `drivers:purge` see it.
5. **Half days.** Declaring and cancelling a half day or a stop needs the new permission `incidents:declare` (SUPER_ADMIN and ADMIN by default; formerly `arrears:manage`, which still gates waivers). The day may now be **today or up to 7 days ahead** (after the contract start, never a Sunday), and a new cause `LATE_SIGNING` ("contrat signé après 12 h", half day only, no hours, note optional) joins `ELECTRICITY` and `OTHER` (note required, e.g. an emergency). A "Demi-journée" button sits in the driver page's payments card.

**Consequences.** (+) Mistaken drivers can be cleaned up, half days fit the real cases. (-) A purge cannot be undone and destroys financial records; only the audit summary remains. Backups are the only way back. The first billable day is still the day **after** the contract start ([contracts.md](../business/contracts.md)), so a contract signed after noon gets its half day on that first billable day.
