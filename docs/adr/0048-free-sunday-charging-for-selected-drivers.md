# 0048. Selected drivers charge free on Sunday; NDZela decides, Zella enforces

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-22 · **Builds on:** [ADR-0033](0033-arrears-suspension-in-both-apps.md), [ADR-0040](0040-zella-reads-a-linked-drivers-contract-and-car.md), [ADR-0044](0044-free-staff-charges.md) · **Design:** Zella repository, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-free-sunday-charging-design.md`

## Context

The company wants to reward some fully paid-up drivers with one free charge every Sunday. It is an individual privilege that administrators grant, pause and withdraw from either dashboard, never a rule for all drivers. NDZela owns the drivers, the contracts and the unpaid days; Zella owns the wallet, the charge and the charger commands. The two already talk through shared-key partner endpoints (ADR-0040) and the `ZellaCommand` outbox (ADR-0033).

## Decision

1. **NDZela owns the agreements and the rule.** Table `free_charging_agreements` (V72, country-owned): driver, optional contract, `starts_on`, optional `ends_on`, status `ACTIVE | SUSPENDED | REVOKED`, conditions, who and when. At most one non-revoked agreement per driver (partial unique index); revoking keeps the row. Audit `FREE_AGREEMENT_CREATED`, `UPDATED`, `SUSPENDED`, `RESUMED`, `REVOKED`. Permissions `free_charging:read` and `free_charging:manage` ([roles and permissions](../auth/roles-and-permissions.md)).
2. **One eligibility service** (`FreeChargingEligibility.check(customerId, now)`) answers `eligible` and exactly one reason, checked in this order: `NOT_LINKED`, `NO_AGREEMENT`, `AGREEMENT_SUSPENDED`, `AGREEMENT_NOT_STARTED`, `AGREEMENT_ENDED` (an agreement ending today is still valid), `DRIVER_SUSPENDED` (any driver status other than `ACTIVE`: suspended, blocked, paused `INACTIVE` or resigned `ARCHIVED`; no separate code), `OUTSIDE_WINDOW`, `UNPAID_DAYS`; otherwise `ELIGIBLE`. The window (Sunday 12:00 included to 22:00 excluded, in the driver's country time zone) lives in one place, `FreeChargingWindow`, configured by `app.free-charging.day|from|until` (`FREE_CHARGING_DAY`, `FREE_CHARGING_FROM`, `FREE_CHARGING_UNTIL`). Saturday's rent is due Sunday at 12:00 (ADR-0038), so at 12:00 a paid-up driver has zero unpaid days.
3. **Unpaid days are computed live** at `now` from the ledger (`AccountService.state(country, contract, now)`), never read from the cached `drivers.unpaid_days` column, which the arrears job refreshes only once a minute and would still say 0 at 12:00:00. One unpaid day removes the benefit; at three the driver is suspended in both apps (ADR-0033) and the answer becomes `DRIVER_SUSPENDED`; paying everything and the next arrears run restore the driver and the benefit.
4. **The shared contract.** `GET /api/v1/partners/zella/customers/{customerId}/free-charging` with `X-Api-Key` (the shared `ZELLA_PARTNER_KEY`; empty key refuses everything; rate limited per Zella customer, 30 per minute, under a ceiling of 2000 per minute for all customers together, then 422 `TOO_MANY_REQUESTS`, so one customer's display traffic cannot starve another customer's free start; Zella also caches the display answer 30 seconds and never caches the start check) answers `{eligible, reason, agreementId, sundayDate, windowEndsAt}`; the last three are null unless eligible. Zella keeps no window or Sunday rule: it uses `sundayDate` as the claim key and `windowEndsAt` as the stop time. A wrong or missing key is 401 with no data.
5. **Zella enforces and holds the one-per-Sunday claim.** At start Zella asks NDZela, then inserts a `free_sunday_claim` row; two partial unique indexes over `CLAIMED` rows, one on (customer, Sunday) and one on (agreement, Sunday) (Zella V45), are the final guarantee against a second use, even with parallel starts and even when one driver has two linked Zella customers (V57 allows a second provider-proven account), because both resolve to the same agreement. The guarantee is per agreement: an administrator who revokes an agreement and creates a new one for the same driver on the same Sunday gets a new agreement id, so that driver could take a second free charge that day. This is an audited administrator action and was accepted rather than keying the claim on the driver. The free start reserves a waived hold at the **server's maximum budget** whatever the app sent, so the budget guard never cuts a free charge (no kWh or money cap).
6. **Releasing the Sunday only on positive evidence that nothing was used:** the start fails, the session never started, or its measured energy is below 0.05 kWh. **Unknown (null) energy keeps the claim**: a driver may lose a Sunday in a rare abandoned session, but never gets two free charges (secure over generous). A free charge stopped early with energy uses the Sunday.
7. **22:00 hard stop.** Zella's sweeper stops every still-active free session whose `windowEndsAt` has passed, through its normal stop path, with stop reason `FREE_WINDOW_ENDED`, at most 5 stops per country per 15-second sweep, retried every sweep while refused, for windows that ended within the last 24 hours (after that the operator stops it by hand).
8. **Management from both dashboards.** Staff use `/api/v1/free-charging/agreements` and `/usage`. The Zella dashboard calls Zella, which forwards to `/api/v1/partners/zella/free-charging/...` with the shared key and `X-Actor`; NDZela runs the call as `zella:<actor>` so the audit trail and `created_by`/`updated_by` name the Zella administrator. From Zella an agreement is created with the Zella `customerId`: NDZela resolves the driver through a **LINKED** id tag only (a suggested or unlinked tag gives `FREE_AGREEMENT_NOT_LINKED`). The usage history lists sessions marked `FREE_SUNDAY`.
9. **Feed category.** Zella marks a free Sunday session `freeCategory = FREE_SUNDAY`. NDZela normalises every incoming category (trim, upper case); an unknown value is stored as `OTHER` with a warning, so a new Zella value never breaks the feed against the V73 check constraint. `FREE_SUNDAY` shows "Offert" and is never money collected (ADR-0044).
10. **Fail closed.** If NDZela is unreachable, slow (Zella: 3 s connect, 5 s read) or answers anything unusable, Zella gives no free benefit and the charge continues as an ordinary paid one. Paid charging is never blocked by this feature. A Zella customer who is not a linked NDZela driver gets `NOT_LINKED` inside Zella and **no request reaches NDZela**.

## Consequences

(+) One rules engine and one set of agreements; the dashboards and the app show reason codes and hold no rule. A second free use of the same Sunday under one agreement is refused by the database (two unique indexes on the Zella claim), across parallel starts and across the linked Zella accounts of one driver; only a revoke-and-recreate on the same Sunday (audited) gives a new agreement and so a new claim. Every administrative action and every use is audited on the side that did it.

(-) Free charging needs NDZela up at the moment of the start. A free session costs the company the electricity and creates no wallet or commission entry.

**Known risks the owner accepted:**
- A charger may ignore the 22:00 stop: energy flows free until it obeys. Stops are retried every sweep within the OCPP quota (60 requests per minute per key); the Zella runbook says when to stop it by hand.
- A driver can lend their Zella account to someone else on Sunday; the same caveat as ADR-0033 (the car is not identified).
- Zella's pre-existing stop path writes the session state without a version check, so a stop racing a just-completed session can, in a window of milliseconds, overwrite it. The window stopper re-reads the session right before each stop; the shared path was left unchanged.
- If Zella crashes between taking the claim and saving the session, the claim has no session: Zella's sweeper releases such orphan claims after 10 minutes (long enough for a start still waiting on the wallet lock), so the driver gets the Sunday back; a start that finds its claim released just after taking the waived hold discards that hold and continues as a paid charge.
- If NDZela is unreachable or over its rate limit at the moment of the start, the free start silently becomes an ordinary paid charge (fail closed, no free/paid flag on the start answer).
- Production must check `select distinct free_category from session_payments` before V73 adds the check constraint.

Alternatives rejected: Zella owning the program with NDZela pushing unpaid days (stale copies, contract rules moved into Zella); each dashboard keeping its own list (duplicated data, certain disagreement).
